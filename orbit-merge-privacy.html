Orbit Merge Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Orbit Merge is an offline, single-player game by gameplaycomesfirst. The current release is intended for ages 13 and older. It does not require an account and does not contain advertising or in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your current round, score, best score, settings, and planet positions in the app's local storage. This information stays on your device. The app does not send it to us or to a game server.

Device access and sharing

The current release does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files. It does not request network access. We do not collect or share personal information through the game.

Removing local data

You can remove saved game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Cloud save is not available in the current release.

Future changes

If we add advertising, purchases, analytics, or other online services, we will update this policy before releasing those features.

Contact

Questions about Orbit Merge or this policy: gameplaycomesfirst.support@gmail.com.
