Orbit Merge Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026. Internal test builds use Google test ads and offer online high scores. Some builds also let you choose a game name. Earlier builds may have these features disabled.

Orbit Merge is a single-player game by gameplaycomesfirst for ages 13 and older. Playing does not require your real name, email address, or a visible account.

Local game data

The game saves your current round, score, best score, sound setting, and planet positions on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes that local data.

Global scores

When you open global scores or finish a scored round in a build with online scores enabled, the app creates an anonymous Firebase identity. Opening global scores fetches the leaderboard. Finishing a scored round sends your score and a random round identifier to Firebase. In builds with a name picker, you can choose from 20 preset game names. Your choice and a randomly generated six-character code are saved on your device. Changing that choice does not contact Firebase. The choice and code are sent with a completed-round score; our backend stores them only when the score is a new personal best. The backend also stores the anonymous identity identifier and recent submission time and keeps the top ten scores. The public board shows the chosen game name, code, and score if the player reaches the top ten. Older entries can still show a generated Explorer label. It does not show your Google profile name, email, or full identity identifier. Scores come from the game client and are not verified competitive results.

Firebase App Check uses Play Integrity attestation to protect the score service. Firebase and its Cloud Functions process technical information such as IP addresses and app attestation data to operate and protect these services. Anonymous Firebase accounts are configured for automatic cleanup after 30 days; leaderboard records may remain after account cleanup. Contact us to request removal of an online score or related record.

Advertising and choices

The free version may show a bottom banner and an interstitial after a completed round. Google AdMob and its SDK can collect and share IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. The app removes Android's advertising ID permission, so the SDK does not receive the Android advertising ID through that permission. We request under-age-of-consent treatment for all ad requests and limit ad content to a PG rating. The app uses Google's consent system and shows an ad privacy choices control when required in your region. Ad availability and choices can vary by location.

Remove ads purchase

The Remove ads purchase is not enabled in the current test build. When offered, Google Play handles its payment information. The app sends a Play purchase token to our Firebase backend so it can ask Google Play whether the purchase is valid. We do not receive your card details. After a verified purchase, the app stops requesting the banner and post-round ads on that installation. Restore purchase checks the current Play account again.

Delete your online score and data

To request deletion of your online score, chosen game name, anonymous player record, or other related data we control, email gameplaycomesfirst.support@gmail.com with the subject "Orbit Merge data deletion." Include your displayed game name and code or generated Explorer label if you have it. We may ask for enough information to locate the correct record, but never for a password, payment card, or verification code. We will confirm when the request is handled. You can remove local game data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

Retention

Best-score and top-ten records are kept until removed by us or replaced through normal leaderboard use. Anonymous Firebase accounts are configured for automatic cleanup after 30 days, but that cleanup does not automatically remove score records. Google and Firebase retain service data under their own policies.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: gameplaycomesfirst.support@gmail.com.
