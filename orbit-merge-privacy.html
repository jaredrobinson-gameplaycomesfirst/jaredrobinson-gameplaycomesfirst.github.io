Smiling Planet Merge Privacy Policy

Updated November 21, 2026. This policy covers the Android game, including its testing and public versions. Google Play test builds may show demo ads.

Smiling Planet Merge is a single-player game by gameplaycomesfirst for ages 13 and older. Playing does not require your real name, email address, or a visible account.

Local game data

The game saves your current round, score, best score, sound setting, and planet positions on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes that local data.

Global scores

When you open global scores, verify a purchase, or choose to record a score in a build with online scores enabled, the app creates an anonymous Firebase identity. Opening global scores fetches the leaderboard. Choosing Record score sends your score and a random round identifier to Firebase once any offered ad reward is earned. Choosing Discard ends the round without uploading it. Earlier versions could submit automatically at the end of a round. In builds with a name picker, you can choose from 20 preset game names. Your choice and a randomly generated six-character code are saved on your device. Some Play builds also offer an optional Play Games gamer name. If you choose it, Google Play Games may ask you to sign in or create a gamer profile. The game reads that profile's gamer display name, not your Google Account's email or real-name field. Your choice is saved on this device. Changing it does not contact Firebase. The selected preset name and code, or the selected Play Games gamer name, are sent with a completed-round score; our backend stores the name only when the score is a new personal best. The backend also stores the anonymous identity identifier and recent submission time and keeps the top ten scores. The public board shows the selected name and score if the player reaches the top ten. Older entries can still show a generated Explorer label. It does not show your email or full identity identifier. Scores come from the game client and are not verified competitive results.

Some Play builds offer Sync best with Play Games as a separate choice in Global high scores. If you choose it, Firebase uses a one-time sign-in code to verify your game-specific Play Games player ID. It stores a one-way fingerprint of that ID linked to your anonymous Firebase record so the same Play Games profile can recover your recorded best and rank. The fingerprint stays private. Score sync does not request your email address or save your sign-in code or Google access token.

Firebase App Check uses Play Integrity attestation to protect the score service. Firebase and its Cloud Functions process technical information such as IP addresses and app attestation data to operate and protect these services. Anonymous Firebase accounts are configured for automatic cleanup after 30 days; leaderboard records may remain after account cleanup. Contact us to request removal of an online score or related record.

Advertising and choices

The free game may show a bottom banner. After the first finished round, the game may offer a rewarded ad when you choose to record a score. You can also discard the score. In the updated game you can record for free when no ad is available. Closing a displayed ad before earning the reward leaves the score pending. Older versions may show a post-round interstitial. Google AdMob and its SDK can collect and share IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. The app removes Android's advertising ID permission, so the SDK does not receive the Android advertising ID through that permission. We request under-age-of-consent treatment for all ad requests and limit ad content to a PG rating. The app uses Google's consent system and shows an ad privacy choices control when required in your region. Ad availability and choices can vary by location.

Remove ads purchase

Remove Ads is an optional one-time purchase where offered. Google Play processes the payment. The app sends a Play purchase token to our Firebase backend so it can ask Google Play whether the purchase is valid. We keep a one-way fingerprint of the purchase token, the product, its verified ownership or refund status, and the date that status changed in Firestore. We use this record to verify access, reconcile refunds, and prevent a refunded purchase from being restored. The ledger contains no payment card details, raw purchase token, or leaderboard name. After a verified purchase, the app stops requesting the banner and post-round ads on that installation. To restore on another device, install the game using the same purchasing Play account and choose Restore purchase.

Delete your online score and data

To request deletion of your online score, chosen game name, anonymous player record, or other related data we control, email gameplaycomesfirst.support@gmail.com with the subject "Smiling Planet Merge data deletion." Include your displayed game name, its code if shown, or a generated Explorer label if you have one. We may ask for enough information to locate the correct record, but never for a password, payment card, or verification code. We will confirm when the request is handled. You can remove local game data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

Retention

Best-score and top-ten records are kept until removed by us or replaced through normal leaderboard use. Anonymous Firebase accounts are configured for automatic cleanup after 30 days, but that cleanup does not automatically remove score records. We retain purchase verification records to support restoration and refunds for the life of the purchase service. Google and Firebase retain service data under their own policies.
