I Hate Bananas privacy policy

Effective November 3, 2026. Developer: gameplaycomesfirst.

Information used by the game

I Hate Bananas runs offline and does not require a game account. The game does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. It contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.

Your campaign progress, tutorial progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. The game does not upload these saves to a server and disables Android's automatic app-data backup. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its local data.

Google Play

If you obtain the game through Google Play, Google handles the store transaction and delivery under its own policies. The game does not receive your payment-card details. Google Play may also perform installation, licensing and app-protection checks under its own policies. Version 0.20.0 contains no in-app purchases.

Audience

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older. It does not request or collect a player's age.

Contacting support

If you choose to contact support, your email address and the information you include in your message are used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the same address; information may need to be retained where required by law or to resolve an ongoing support request.

Contact: gameplaycomesfirst.support@gmail.com.

Policy changes

This policy will be updated if the game's data practices change. Any future online service, advertising, analytics or in-app purchase feature will need to be described before it is introduced.
