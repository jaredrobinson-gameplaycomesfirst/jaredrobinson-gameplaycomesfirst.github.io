Delete your Odd Alliance wallet account

Odd Alliance is developed by Jared Robinson under the gameplaycomesfirst brand. Wallet-enabled versions offer optional Google sign-in for Renown and earned DLC.

In the app, open Renown Wallet → Delete Wallet Account, confirm, and sign in again to verify ownership. This permanently deletes your Renown balance, earned DLC entitlements, reward records, and game wallet account.

If you cannot access the app, email gameplaycomesfirst.support@gmail.com with the subject “Odd Alliance wallet account deletion,” using the Google email address associated with your wallet. We will verify ownership before deleting the account. Never send passwords or verification codes. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.

Your local saved match and Google Play purchases are separate. Account deletion does not cancel or refund Play purchases, delete your Google account, or delete advertising/billing records held independently by Google. Clearing app storage removes local app data.

A minimal hashed deletion marker is retained for seven days to block stale authentication tokens and is then removed by daily cleanup, normally within eight days. Support correspondence is deleted 90 days after the request is resolved. See the privacy policy.
