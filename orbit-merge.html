Smiling Planet Merge

Launch planets toward a little sun. Merge matching planets, build chains, and see how high you can score before the arena fills up.

Smiling Planet Merge, formerly Orbit Merge, is a single-player Android puzzle game by gameplaycomesfirst.

Open the Google Play test

How Play Games is used

You can play with a preset name. In supported builds, you can also choose to use your Google Play Games gamer name. Your chosen name can appear on the global leaderboard with a qualifying score. Smiling Planet Merge does not receive your Google Account email address through this feature.
